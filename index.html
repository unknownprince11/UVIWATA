Who we are

Childcare providers, stronger together.

UVIWATA is an independent, membership-based association bringing together daycare centre owners and childcare providers. It supports collective representation, professional learning, childcare quality and sustainable childcare enterprises.

Get to know UVIWATA →
✳

We work within Tanzania's laws and applicable standards. Registration, inspection and regulatory oversight remain the responsibility of designated government authorities.

Why childcare matters

A strong start makes room for everyone to thrive.

Quality childcare supports children's wellbeing and creates opportunity for families, care professionals and local enterprises.

Children

Nurturing care can help children learn, explore and build foundations for life.

Families

Reliable childcare helps parents and caregivers balance care with daily life and work.

Livelihoods

Skilled childcare professionals contribute to stronger communities and local jobs.

Enterprises

Sustainable childcare businesses support families while creating economic opportunity.

Care in our communities

When childcare is supported, families and communities can thrive.

Childcare connects children's wellbeing with the everyday lives, livelihoods and opportunities of the people around them.
